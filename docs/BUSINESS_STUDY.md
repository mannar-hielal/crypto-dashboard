## The Honest Reality

You are an individual in Switzerland, unregistered, with a product still in development. That shapes everything.

**You cannot:**

- Hold client funds
- Offer financial advice
- Call yourself a broker or exchange
- Guarantee trading performance

**You can:**

- Build a tool, charge for access to that tool
- Earn referral income from partners (Bybit affiliate)

That is a clean, legal, low-risk position — and it's actually where the money is.

## Revenue Streams

### Stream 1 — Bybit Affiliate Program _(Start Here, Zero Cost)_

This is your fastest path to income. Requires no registration, no customers, and no finished product.

Bybit pays affiliates a percentage of trading fees generated by referred users — **for life**. A single active trader doing $100K/month in volume generates fees you share in perpetually.

**How to activate:**

- Register at [partner.bybit.com](https://partner.bybit.com) as an individual
- Switzerland is eligible
- You get a referral link and a dashboard showing earnings

**Where it fits in your app:**
Every screen that mentions "go live" or "connect your real account" should carry your affiliate link. Your app is the perfect funnel — users paper trade, get comfortable, then go live on Bybit through your link.

**Realistic income:**

- 10 active referred traders at moderate volume = CHF 200–800/month passively
- 100 users = meaningful recurring income

### Stream 2 — Freemium SaaS _(Your Main Play, Phase 2 Onwards)_

Your app already has the depth to justify a paid tier.

**Free tier** gives users enough to get hooked — 1 chart, market orders, basic paper trading, 3 symbols.

**Paid tier ($9–15/month)** unlocks what you've already built — 4-panel layout, all symbols, bracket orders, TP/SL monitor, sound alerts, journal, balance history.

**Payment as an individual in Switzerland:**
You don't need to register a company. Use **Stripe** or **Lemon Squeezy** as an individual with your Swiss bank account. Lemon Squeezy acts as the "merchant of record" — they handle VAT across all countries on your behalf. You receive clean payouts. This is legal and common for indie developers.

**Realistic income:**

- 50 paying users at $12/month = $600/month
- 200 users = $2,400/month

## Legal Position — Switzerland Specific

- As an individual in Switzerland earning from a software tool, you can earn freelance/side income without registering a company up to a reasonable threshold. Above roughly **CHF 100K/year** you'd want to formalize — but that's a good problem to have.
- Declare income in your Swiss tax return as **self-employment income** (_Erwerbseinkommen aus selbständiger Erwerbstätigkeit_). Keep records of all earnings.
- Your app must have clear disclaimers: _"This is a paper trading simulator for educational purposes only. Not financial advice. Past simulated performance does not guarantee future results."_
- You do **not** need a FINMA license — you are not handling real money, not operating an exchange, and not providing regulated financial advice. You are a software tool, exactly like TradingView.
- **GDPR applies** since you serve EU users. With your current architecture (Lucia Auth, etc.) you're already handling this well — just maintain a privacy policy page.
