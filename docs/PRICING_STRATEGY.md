# Pricing Strategy — Which Model to Use

> Goal: Make cryptopapertrade.com the go-to tool for crypto traders — specialized in crypto, no stocks or options noise.

---

## The Goal Shapes the Model

"Go-to for crypto traders" is a positioning statement that immediately tells you what the model must **NOT** be. It cannot be aggressive, friction-heavy, or feel like a trap. Traders are sharp, skeptical people who compare tools constantly and talk to each other. The model has to **feel fair** or it becomes a negative talking point on Twitter and Reddit before you have 100 users.

---

## Models Evaluated

### Model 1 — Freemium (Free Tier + Paid Tiers) ✅ Recommended

**How it works:** A genuinely useful free tier exists permanently. Paid tiers unlock more power. No time pressure, no trial expiry.

**Pros:**

- Traders can use the tool, get comfortable, and upgrade when they feel ready — no pressure
- Free users become your marketing — every paper trader who tweets about a winning setup is free acquisition
- Word of mouth in crypto communities works on free tools, not on "sign up for a trial" tools
- Bybit affiliate income works even on free users — they may go live on Bybit regardless of their tier
- Lower churn because paid users chose to upgrade, they weren't pushed

**Cons:**

- Slower initial revenue — you need volume before the conversion math works
- You have to be disciplined about what goes in the free tier (too generous = nobody upgrades; too stingy = nobody stays)

---

### Model 2 — 14-Day All-Inclusive Trial Then Paywall ❌ Not Recommended

**How it works:** Full access for 14 days, then a hard wall requiring payment to continue.

**Pros:**

- Faster initial revenue if conversion rate is decent
- Forces users to experience the full product before deciding

**Cons:**

- Traders who paper trade are building habits over **weeks and months**. A 14-day window cuts them off exactly when they are starting to develop conviction about the tool
- The day the trial ends with an open position in their journal, they feel trapped — that emotion converts badly; they either pay resentfully or abandon
- Crypto Twitter and Reddit will describe your app as a "14-day trial trap" in comparisons — that label sticks
- You lose the free-user word-of-mouth flywheel entirely
- Hard to combine with affiliate income — free users who would have referred Bybit signups just leave

> **Honest verdict:** This model works for B2B SaaS where the buyer is a company with a budget. It works poorly for individual traders who are inherently price-sensitive and comparison-driven.

---

### Model 3 — One Price, All Features ❌ Not Recommended as primary

**How it works:** One subscription price, everything included. No tiers.

**Pros:**

- Simple to communicate
- No "am I on the right tier" confusion

**Cons:**

- You lose the upgrade path — a user who would have started free and upgraded after 3 months of love has to make a cold purchase decision on day one
- Kills the viral growth loop entirely
- Harder to segment — you cannot offer a lower price to people who need less

---

### Model 4 — Lifetime Deal (One-Time Payment) ✅ As a launch strategy only

**How it works:** Pay once, own it forever. Usually done at launch to generate initial cash.

**Pros:**

- Immediate cash during the most cash-poor phase of the project
- Creates loyal early users who feel ownership and give honest feedback
- Great for launching on Product Hunt or AppSumo

**Cons:**

- Does not build recurring revenue
- Lifetime deal buyers expect you to keep building forever with no ongoing income from them
- If done too early, you lock in a price that may be too low as the product grows

> **Recommendation:** Use a lifetime deal as a **launch strategy for the first 60–90 days**, then switch to subscriptions permanently.

---

## Recommended Model: Freemium + Two Paid Tiers + Launch Lifetime Deal

---

### Free — The Hook

The free tier must be **genuinely useful**. Not crippled, not a demo. A trader should be able to use it daily and get real value. This fills the top of your funnel.

**What Free gets:**

- Single chart panel (1×1 layout only)
- 3 symbols (BTC, ETH, SOL)
- 3 intervals (1m, 15m, 1h)
- EMA 9 and EMA 200 only
- Paper trading with **market orders only** (no limit, no stop)
- Basic positions tab
- 7-day order history
- 10,000 USDT starting balance, reset once per week
- Leverage capped at 1× (no leverage on free)
- PWA installable

**What Free does NOT get:**

- Multi-chart layouts
- All 9 symbols
- All 6 intervals
- MACD, VWAP, EMA 20
- Limit and stop orders
- Bracket orders (TP/SL)
- Sound alerts
- Trading journal
- Balance history
- Edit panels
- Custom leverage
- Unlimited resets

---

### Pro — CHF 9/month — The Core Product

This is where **80% of your paying users** will land. Price it to feel like an obvious yes.

**Everything in Free, plus:**

- All 4 chart layouts (2h, 2v, 3h, 4-panel)
- All 9 symbols
- All 6 intervals
- All indicators (EMA 9/20/200, MACD, VWAP)
- Limit and stop orders
- Bracket orders (TP/SL on all order types)
- Edit panels for positions and pending orders
- Sound alerts
- Full trading journal (200 entries)
- Balance history
- Leverage up to 10×
- Unlimited account resets
- 90-day order history
- Bybit affiliate link integration (when they click "go live")

---

### Pro+ — CHF 19/month — The Power Tier

> This tier **does not need to exist on day one.** Build it when Phase 4 and Phase 6 features are ready. Placeholder it now, deliver it later.

**Everything in Pro, plus:**

- AI trading bot (Phase 6 — supervised mode)
- Advanced indicators (RSI, Bollinger Bands, ATR — Phase 4)
- Multi-symbol watchlist (Phase 4)
- Price alerts
- ATM-style bracket strategies
- Strategy templates
- Priority support
- Early access to new features

---

## Launch Strategy — Lifetime Deal

For the first **60–90 days** after public launch, offer a lifetime Pro deal at **CHF 49**.

**Promote on:**

- Product Hunt launch
- r/algotrading, r/CryptoCurrency, r/TradingView
- Crypto Twitter with a demo video
- Your waitlist email list

**Goal:** 100 lifetime deals = CHF 4,900 upfront. This funds server costs for 2 years and gives you a base of invested early users who will report bugs, suggest features, and tell their trading friends.

After the lifetime deal period ends, **remove it permanently** and switch to monthly subscriptions only. Make this clear from the start: _"Founding member pricing, available for the first 60 days only."_

## The Number That Matters

| Milestone                      | MRR                    |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------- |
| 200 Pro subscribers @ CHF 9/mo | CHF 1,800/month        |
| 500 Pro subscribers @ CHF 9/mo | CHF 4,500/month        |
| 500 Pro + Bybit affiliate      | CHF 5,000–6,000+/month |

200 paying subscribers is a realistic target within **12 months** of public launch for a niche tool with genuine quality, a free tier driving organic growth, and consistent community presence.

The free tier is not a charity — it is your **acquisition engine**. Every free user who installs the PWA and paper trades daily is a potential paying subscriber, a potential Bybit affiliate referral, and a potential person who tells their trading group about your tool.
